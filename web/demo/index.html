Things to try

  • Home: press Feed now, then press it again to see the cooldown refuse a second feed.
  • Schedule: add a feeding time a minute or two from now and watch it appear in History.
  • Network: scan and connect to a network. The simulated router accepts the password fishfood.
  • Feeders: Goldfish is calibrated, so its portions show in grams; Tetras is not. Press Calibrate to see how it is done.
  • Settings: switch to the light theme.

Nothing is saved: reloading the page starts again. Firmware updates are not part of the demo.
